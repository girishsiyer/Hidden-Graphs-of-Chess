What is Graph Chess?

Chess can be viewed not only as pieces and squares, but also as a system of relationships.

Graph Chess explores these relationships using graph-based thinking to understand chess positions and support the process of finding good moves.

The Three Graphs

PG — Pawn Graph

The Pawn Graph represents the structure and relationships created by pawns on the chessboard.

MG — Movement Graph

The Movement Graph represents movement possibilities and flexibility of the pieces.

CG — Connection Graph

The Connection Graph represents relationships and support between pieces.
