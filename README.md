# Hidden Graphs of Chess

**A graph-based method to learn and play chess.**

Hidden Graphs of Chess explores chess positions through three graph structures:

* **Pawn Graph (PG)** — represents relationships and structures formed by pawns.
* **Mobility Graph (MG)** — represents the roles and flexibility of non-pawn pieces.
* **Coordination Graph (CG)** — represents coordination and support between pieces.

The purpose of Graph Chess is not to replace traditional chess concepts, but to provide another way to understand a position and make decisions.

## Articles

The project is being developed through a series of articles that explain the ideas, structures, and applications of Graph Chess.

Recent topics include:

* Graph Chess and its basic structures
* Mobility Graph and piece flexibility
* Graph Chess and Initiative
* Weaknesses in chess topologies
* Chess positions versus chess topology

Each article develops a particular aspect of the method while keeping the underlying system accessible to chess players.

## Graph Chess and Traditional Chess

Traditional chess uses concepts such as initiative, attack, defence, mobility, space, weaknesses, and coordination.

Graph Chess investigates whether some of these concepts can be understood through the structure and transformation of graphs.

The objective is not to create new terminology for its own sake, but to develop a practical framework for analysing positions.

## Research

This repository contains the public development of the **Hidden Graphs of Chess** project.

The method is still being developed and tested. The current version focuses on establishing the basic framework and explaining how the three graphs can be used to analyse chess positions.

More advanced ideas and experimental work may be added as the research develops.
